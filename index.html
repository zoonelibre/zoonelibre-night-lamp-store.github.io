Frequently asked questions

Are the prices real?

Yes. The catalog uses retail reference prices researched from the linked retailer listings on September 29, 2026. Retail prices, promotions and stock can change at any time.

Are the product images real?

The storefront loads live previews of the linked official retailer product pages. This keeps the demo connected to the real product listing instead of packaging third-party photography inside the ZIP.

Can customers pay on this site?

The included cart works locally, but checkout is intentionally a demo. Connect a real payment and order platform such as Stripe, PayPal, Shopify or another provider before accepting orders.

Can I change products and prices?

Yes. Edit products.js to add, remove or update products, prices, categories, colors and source links.
